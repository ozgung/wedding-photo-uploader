Your memories and information

{{EVENT_TITLE}} Wedding Photos is a private photo and video collection service operated by the wedding organizer for a family album. Contact for the site and album: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Information collected and its purpose

We process your selected photos and videos, filenames, file sizes, upload times and status, and the name you optionally provide. Original files are preserved, including any embedded date, device or location metadata. We use this information to collect and organize the family album and retry interrupted transfers.

Google Drive access

Only the album organizer authorizes a Google account; guests do not sign in to Google. The app uses the limited drive.file permission to manage files it creates or that are explicitly made available to it. It processes Drive file and folder identifiers, sizes and integrity information to confirm transfers. It does not request access to the whole Drive account. Authorization credentials are stored privately on the server.

Storage and sharing

Files are temporarily uploaded to our server at {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}, then transferred to the organizer’s Google Drive. Confirmed temporary copies are deleted. Incomplete uploads are cleaned up after 24 hours without activity; files awaiting transfer or review are retained until resolved. The Drive album is retained as a family archive until the organizer or couple deletes it. The couple and album organizer can access the files; uploads are not published on this website.

Security and providers

The upload site uses necessary session cookies and Cloudflare and Turnstile for bot protection. Providers may process IP addresses, browser and technical connection information when handling requests. We retain upload-status records and technical error logs on the server. This service uses no advertising or marketing analytics; information is not sold or used to train AI models.

Deletion and contact

To request deletion of a photo or your name, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with details such as the approximate upload time and filename so we can locate it. We may need to verify that the request comes from the relevant person. Respect other guests’ privacy when uploading. The organizer can revoke the app’s Drive access in Google account settings; revocation does not automatically delete files already saved in Drive.

Provider privacy information: Google · Cloudflare · {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}
